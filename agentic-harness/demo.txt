|---|----------|------|----------|------------|
| 1 | **High** | heap-buffer-overflow WRITE (~64 KiB) | `parse_data` canary.c:101 | 16-bit integer overflow on `size * count` truncates `malloc` size while loop bound uses full 32-bit product |

All four PoCs are minimal (9–13 bytes), deterministic, and reproduce 3/3 in fresh sandboxes. Reports saved under `results/canary/`.

Key recurring weakness: the parser repeatedly **trusts header-declared length fields** (`size`, `count`, `name_len`, `e->length`) without validating them against the actual allocation or remaining input — both during input-buffer clamping and inside individual entry handlers.
